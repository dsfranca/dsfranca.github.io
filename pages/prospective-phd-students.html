Prospective PhD Students

Thank you for your interest in pursuing a PhD with me. I can only recruit PhD students through official calls administered by the University of Copenhagen, and I cannot accept PhD students outside that process.

Open positions, eligibility requirements, deadlines, and application instructions are published on the Department of Mathematical Sciences PhD application page. Please apply through the official call described there. A direct email to me cannot replace the formal application.
